Why kitchen remodel quotes can be hard to compare

Two proposals can describe the same project very differently. One may include demolition, debris removal, permits, flooring installation, or electrical work while another leaves those items out or does not state them clearly. Allowances can also make totals look similar even when the amount set aside for cabinets, countertops, fixtures, or flooring is very different.

ScopeMatch does not decide which contractor is better and does not estimate a “fair” price. It organizes the information you enter so you can see where the proposals differ and what to clarify before signing.

What should I compare besides total price?

Look for demolition, disposal, cabinetry, countertops, plumbing, electrical work, flooring, finishing, permits, inspections, warranties, and change-order terms. Mark an item Unclear only when the quote genuinely does not state it.

Does ScopeMatch recommend a contractor?

No. It compares only information you enter. It does not rank contractors, judge workmanship, verify licensing, determine appropriate scope, or replace professional advice.
